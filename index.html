FAQ

Common questions

Do we need a technical team?

No. If you can use email, WhatsApp or a spreadsheet, you can run what I build, and I train you on it.

Which tools do you work with?

Gmail/Outlook, Slack, HubSpot, Notion, Google Sheets, Shopify, Zapier/Make, WhatsApp Business and custom APIs. If it has an integration or API, it can usually be connected.

Is our data safe?

I use least-privilege access, document every integration, and won't use your private data to train public models without your written approval. See the privacy section below.

How fast can we see results?

Most first automations take 2–4 weeks after the audit. Simple ones can be faster.

What if an automation breaks?

Starter includes 2 weeks of post-launch support. Growth and Scale include ongoing monitoring and fixes.

Can we start with just one process?

Yes, and I recommend it. Pick the most painful workflow, prove the value, then expand.
